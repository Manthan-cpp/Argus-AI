# Submission Details & Video Storyboard

## Hackathon Submission Metadata
- **Project Name:** Argus — Real-Time Assistive Safety Workflows for Cameras
- **Tagline:** Turn any camera into a reactive safety workflow: define rules in plain sentences, track pose on-device, and escalate incidents with Serverpod.
- **Backend:** Serverpod 4 (Dart) + Postgres
- **Frontend:** Flutter Web + MediaPipe Vision (WASM)
- **AI Tools Disclosed:** Google Antigravity, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash / Flash Lite for NLP parsing & vision verification.

## Video Storyboard (< 2 Minutes)
1. **0:00 - 0:15:** Problem statement — WHO fall statistics, blind spots in workplace monitoring, surveillance cameras that nobody watches.
2. **0:15 - 0:40:** Rule Studio Demo — Type a natural-language rule: *"If someone falls near the staircase and stays down for 20s, alert supervisor"*. Watch it parse into an animated visual workflow graph.
3. **0:40 - 1:10:** Live Monitor & Incident Firing — Scenario S1 & S2 live detection. Bounding boxes, pose skeleton, zone entry, fall detection, automatic blurred-evidence capture.
4. **1:10 - 1:35:** Incident Workflow & Escalation — Incident timeline, Serverpod real-time streaming to supervisor dashboard, escalation countdown timer, one-click acknowledgment.
5. **1:35 - 1:50:** Detector Lab & Privacy — Transparent precision/recall benchmarks, on-device WASM inference proof, zero raw video sent to cloud.
6. **1:50 - 2:00:** Serverpod Architecture recap & closing.
